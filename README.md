### 🔭 About me

I work at the **intersection of Physics and Computing** — developing software and simulations to solve real-world problems. I create tools and methods to tackle complex challenges, making the work of researchers and scientists around the world a little easier.

I'm a strong advocate for **open-source code** and the **Open Science model**, actively contributing to open and collaborative projects. Coding is how I think, and building is how I create.
